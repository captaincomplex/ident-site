Ident reads your roster and keeps up-to-date

Anyone who knows an airline pilot understands that their day can change in an instant. The best-laid plans for your time together can change drastically depending on all sorts of factors. Sometimes, by the time they can tell you, it's too late.

Ident gives an instant oversight of how their plans change, as it happens. No more mental maths or waiting for a text back when they land: if they are delayed, the wall shows it.

Your roster never leaves the device.

  1. Your airlinepublishes your roster
  2. Your Identfetches it over your own connection and keeps it there
  3. The wallshows only what you choose

We never receive it. Only the current flight number goes to a tracking service, and a home location to Google Maps if you switch live traffic on.

What’s inside.

Three ways to build it, drawn apart. Nothing proprietary and nothing soldered. The drawings are schematic and not to scale.

Raspberry Pi and e-paper

  1. Frame or mount. The window is cut to the panel; the frame setting draws the board inside whatever window your mount leaves.
  2. Pimoroni Inky Impression 7.3″. 800×480, six colours (Spectra 6), four buttons on the side.
  3. Raspberry Pi. A Pi 3 Model A+ or Pi Zero 2 WH. The 40-pin header pushes straight onto the back of the panel: no soldering, no wiring.
  4. Battery, optional. A PiSugar 3 clips on underneath and leaves the display connector free.
  5. Stand or wall mount.
  6. microSD card. A1-rated, 16–32 GB, with Ident on it.
  7. Power. The official 12.5 W micro-USB supply.

The reference build. Parts and prices

Raspberry Pi and LED matrix

  1. Two 64×32 HUB75 panels, chained into one 128×32 board.
  2. Ribbon cable. Carries the picture from the bonnet to the first panel; the panels chain to each other.
  3. Adafruit RGB Matrix Bonnet, or the Matrix HAT + RTC, pushed onto the Pi.
  4. Raspberry Pi 3 Model A+.
  5. 5 V 4 A panel supply. Separate from the Pi’s, wired straight into the bonnet. Never through the Pi.
  6. The Pi’s own supply. 12.5 W micro-USB.

We take privacy seriously.

We never see your roster or give anyone else access to it — only the current flight number.  You can choose to use a calendar link or an offline copy of your roster. During setup you can choose to enable live traffic information, if you provide your home location — this is only sent to Google Maps. You control your Ident on your home WiFi network, securely.

You have control.

Sensitive roster codes such as FTGD, UNFT, NSO, SOC, MEET, WFTU, HOSP or RESN can be automatically hidden — these will then show as days off or standby, depending on your preference. It isn't just hidden on screen: those codes are discarded when your roster is read, so they never reach the device's storage at all. Nothing that isn't kept can appear later.
