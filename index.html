05 / Privacy

Your roster never leaves the house.

  1. Your airlinepublishes your roster
  2. Your Identfetches it over your own connection and keeps it there
  3. The wallshows only what you choose

We never receive your roster. If you switch on flight status, each flight's number, departure airport and time go through our server to Flightradar24, from 20 minutes before departure until it lands. If you switch on drive times, your home location (or a nearby corner, if you prefer) goes through our server to Google Maps on the way home. Neither is kept. Everything that leaves your display.

Sickness, compassionate leave, fatigue, a meeting: sensitive roster codes such as FTGD, UNFT, SOC or MEET are hidden by default and show as an ordinary day off. They're discarded when your roster is read, so they never reach the device's storage. You choose which, if any, to show.

06 / Follow the flight

Point a phone at the screen. Watch the flight.

While you're airborne, Ident can show a code that opens your flight live on Flightradar24. Press a button and it fills the screen, big enough to scan from across the room, or keep a small one in the corner.

08 / Setting up

Switch it on, then use your phone.

No keyboard, no monitor. Out of the box the screen shows how to begin. It raises its own Wi-Fi network: join it from your phone, pick your home network, paste your roster link, and the board appears about a minute later.

e-paper isn't instant

About 30 seconds to redraw, so it changes when something changes rather than ticking. Want live movement? Choose LED.
