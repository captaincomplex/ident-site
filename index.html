Know when they're home.

Ident is a small display for a shelf or wall. It reads a pilot's roster and shows the family where they are, when they land and when they'll be through the door. When crewing change the plan, the display changes too.

01 / For most homes

The ready-made 7.3-inch display

A colour e-paper screen in its own case, with a stand and a battery, so it can sit on a shelf with no cable. You set it up from your phone: join it to your Wi-Fi, paste your roster link, and the board appears. It shows the Solari board unless you choose another style.

03 / How it works

Standby, report, airborne, turnaround and heading home all look different on the board.

It times the trip home

The home time allows for debrief, the walk to the car and the drive you set.

04 / Privacy

Your roster is read and kept on the display

We never receive your roster. If you switch on flight status, each flight's number, departure airport and time go through our server to Flightradar24, from 20 minutes before departure until it lands. If you switch on drive times, your home location (or a nearby corner, if you prefer) goes through our server to Google Maps on the way home. Neither is kept.

Sickness, compassionate leave, fatigue and meetings are hidden by default and show as an ordinary day off. You choose which, if any, to show. Everything that leaves your display.
