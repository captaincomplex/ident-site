Airline not supported? Apply for a free Ident

Ident reads easyJet eCrew rosters, and RosterBuster's calendar link for any airline RosterBuster supports (how to set it up). For any other roster, help us read it and the software is yours free, permanently, including everything that comes after.

How it works

You send a sample

One roster, with your name, staff number and crew list taken out. That's enough to see how your airline lays a month out.

We write the parser

Usually a few evenings, with some questions for you about your duty codes.

You test it, and keep it

You run it against your real roster and tell us what's wrong. When it's right, your licence is free, with no expiry.

Your codes, in plain words on the wall

Ident shows each duty in words a family can read. These are the three example codes from the form below.

Renderings drawn by Ident's own software from a sample roster.

What to send

Most useful first.

A calendar feed

If your airline offers an .ics subscription (AIMS eCrew, CrewLink, Sabre and others do), this is the quickest route. Tell us which system it comes from; don't send the link itself. If you use RosterBuster and Ident shows a code it doesn't know, tell us the letters and what they mean.

Or a roster PDF

The monthly one you already download, with your name, staff number and anyone else on it blacked out.

Your duty codes

What your airline calls a day off, standby, positioning, training and sickness. Only you can tell us these, and they take the longest.

Your sample. A person reads it to work out the format. It isn't uploaded anywhere or given to any service, and it is deleted once the parser works, or sooner if you ask. Never send your live calendar link: anyone holding it can read your roster. Once your airline is supported, your roster stays on your own device, as it does for everyone else.

Apply

Replace the examples with your airline's codes, and say which ones you're unsure of. A wrong guess can put a standby on the wall as a flight.
Then email a sample roster, since the form can't carry a file. Attach it to a message to support@xpdr.aero, a month's roster as you download it, with your name, staff number and anyone else on it blacked out: PDF, .ics, HTML or a screenshot of the crew portal, whatever you get.

Before you apply

It might not be possible

Some airlines publish nothing a device can read. If yours is one, we'll tell you.

It takes weeks

Ident is built by a working pilot around their own roster, so expect weeks rather than days, and longer if your airline changes its format part way through.

You still buy your own hardware

The software is free; the display comes from the usual shops. See parts.
