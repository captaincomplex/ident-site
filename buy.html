Paying, renewing and stopping

What you pay

Mode A is £19.99, once. Mode C is £2.99 a month, or £29.99 a year (about £2.50 a month). Mode S is £3.99 a month, or £39.99 a year (about £3.33 a month). That’s all: no VAT (xpdr.aero isn’t VAT-registered), no setup fee, no cancellation fee, no fee for a failed payment.

When it renews

Monthly renews on the same date each month, yearly on the same date each year, at the price you signed up at, until you cancel. If the price ever changes we email you at least 30 days before, and you can cancel first.

Cancel in two clicks

From Manage your subscription, your display’s control panel, or the link in our emails. No questions, no offers to click past. You keep the mode to the end of the month or year you’ve paid for, then the display goes back to Mode A by itself.

14 days to change your mind

Cancel Mode C or S within 14 days of subscribing and we refund everything you paid for it. A yearly plan gets another 14 days after each renewal, with that year refunded. A Mode A licence has its own refund terms.

No nagging

No marketing, and no reminder the law doesn’t require. Monthly: one about every six months, 15 days before a payment. Yearly: two before each renewal, about 30 and 15 days before, and a note on the day it renews that your 14 days have started, all sent by us. Plus a receipt for each payment.

Who you’re buying from

xpdr.aero, in the UK. Stripe handles the card payment, so card details never reach us. Full details in the subscription terms.

Why the names?

They’re transponder modes. Mode A tells air traffic control who you are. Mode C adds your altitude, where you really are. Mode S adds the data link. Ident is named after the button that makes your aircraft stand out on the controller’s screen.

Why a subscription?

Live data is charged to us for every request, every few minutes, for every display. Modes C and S pay for it. Mode A needs none of it.

Why yearly is cheaper

One payment a year costs us less to take than twelve, and you’re with us for longer, so we share that: twelve months for the price of ten.
