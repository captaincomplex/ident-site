Privacy

Last updated 24 August 2026.

The short version

Your roster never reaches us. Ident fetches it from your airline, over your own internet connection, and stores it on your own device. We do not receive it, cannot see it, and operate no service that holds it. There is no account to create and the software sends us no analytics or telemetry.

What your device does

Your display makes outbound connections only to: your roster feed; optionally a flight-tracking service and Google Maps, if you supply keys for them; ident.xpdr.aero, once a day, to check for a software update — that is our domain, served by GitHub Pages, and the request carries nothing but a version check; and once, on first run, to download coastline data for the map styles. None of those requests carry personal data to us.

What we actually hold

Only what you give us directly:

  • If you buy: your name, email address and billing details, handled by our payment provider [PAYMENT PROVIDER], who acts as merchant of record. We keep order records as long as tax law requires.
  • If you email support: your message and anything you attach — including a diagnostics file, if you choose to send one. Diagnostics deliberately exclude your roster contents, your roster URL, API keys, passwords and Wi-Fi passwords; you can read the file in full before sending it.

We use this only to fulfil your order and answer you. We don't sell it or use it for advertising.

Your rights

Under UK GDPR you may ask for a copy of what we hold, ask us to correct or delete it, or object to how we use it. Email support@xpdr.aero. If you're unhappy with our response you can complain to the Information Commissioner's Office at ico.org.uk.

A word to crew

Your roster may be confidential to your airline. Because Ident keeps it on your own hardware this is a far simpler position than a cloud service — but checking your employer's data and IT policies is your responsibility, not ours.

Cookies

This website sets no cookies and runs no analytics. The control panel on your own device uses a single session cookie to keep you logged in, which never leaves your network.
