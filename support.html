Before you write

These three cover the majority of messages.

Wrong date on screen

Both products set their clock from the internet, so a long time without Wi-Fi can leave the date behind. Reconnect it and the clock fixes itself in a minute or two.

Won't join Wi-Fi

On an E1002 or a Pi Zero 2 W the network must be 2.4GHz: neither can see 5GHz at all. Check that before anything else.

Roster not appearing

Check the calendar link is the full .ics URL and hasn't expired. Failing that, export a .ics from the crew portal and upload that instead.
