reTerminal E1002 · E1001

Update over USB-C

Your display normally updates itself over Wi-Fi once a day. If it can't — or you'd rather not wait — you can install the latest version from a computer here. Your Wi-Fi, calendar link and other settings are kept.

Checking which version is available…

What you need

  • A computer with Google Chrome or Microsoft Edge. Phones and iPads can't do this: their browsers can't talk to USB devices.
  • A USB-C cable that carries data, not only charge. If the computer doesn't see the display, try another cable first.
  • On a Mac: Seeed say the display's USB connection needs WCH's CH34x driver, approved afterwards under System Settings → Privacy & Security. See Seeed's getting-started page.

Steps

  1. Plug the display into the computer with the USB-C cable.
  2. Keep it awake: hold any of its three buttons for three seconds, until the screen shows SETUP. Between updates the display is asleep, and a sleeping display can't be updated. It stays awake on the setup screen for 15 minutes.
  3. Click the button for your model below, choose the port that appears, then Install.
  4. When asked Erase device?, leave it unticked. Ticking it wipes your settings and you'd have to set the display up again.
  5. Wait for Installation complete — about a minute or two. The display restarts and shows your board; the screen flashing through its colours as it redraws is normal.

If it doesn't work

  • No port appears: check the driver (Mac), the cable, and that the screen says SETUP.
  • It stops part-way: nothing is lost — the old version stays until the new one is complete. Hold a button for three seconds and try again.
  • Still stuck? Support.
